Privacy Notice

What we collect

For account registration we may collect first name, last name, email address, country and optional phone number. We do not request government ID through the standard registration form. If you connect a wallet, we store the public wallet address you verify. If you register with or generate a referral code, we store that attribution. We also store a one-way cryptographic hash of the IP address used at registration - never the IP address itself - to help detect fraudulent or duplicate accounts in the referral program.

Why we use it

To create and secure accounts, communicate with users, maintain referral attribution, detect and prevent fraud and abuse, provide requested services and meet applicable legal obligations. Optional marketing requires a separate consent choice.

Legal basis and minimisation

Each processing activity should have an appropriate legal basis and the information collected should be limited to what is necessary. We do not use one blanket consent for unrelated purposes.

Retention, recipients and transfers

We retain information only as long as needed for the stated purpose or applicable legal requirements. Service providers may process data on our behalf under appropriate agreements, including public blockchain data providers used to verify transactions and detect referral fraud (a connected wallet address may be queried against public on-chain data for this purpose). If data is transferred outside the EEA, applicable safeguards should be documented.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority.
