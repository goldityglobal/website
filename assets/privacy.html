Privacy Notice

Who we are

GOLDITY (goldityglobal.com) runs this website and the Telegram airdrop bot @GOLDITY_airdrop_bot. Privacy questions and requests: info@goldityglobal.com.

What we collect

Account. First and last name, email address, country, an optional phone number, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), your confirmation that you are at least 18, and the versions of the Terms and Privacy Notice you accepted. We do not request government ID.

Wallets and referrals. The public wallet addresses you connect and verify by signing a message, your referral attribution, and the public on-chain transactions of those wallets that we read to calculate and pay referral rewards.

Website airdrop. Your wallet address, claim status, transaction hash, time of claim and a hash of your IP address.

Telegram airdrop. Your numeric Telegram user ID and chat ID, the wallet address you send, your claim status and the reason if a check failed, the time of your claim, and the steps you take in the bot (such as pressing Start or Claim) so we can see where people get stuck. We do not store your Telegram username, name or phone number.

Support, notifications and Design Vault. Support tickets and messages you send, notifications shown in your account, and the designs you unlock or download together with related points or credit entries.

Security data. To detect fraud, duplicate accounts and abuse, we store a one-way hash of your IP address (and of your browser information) with registration, airdrop claims and security events. We do not store these IP addresses themselves. To limit repeated login, registration and password-reset attempts, IP addresses are held in short-lived counters that are deleted automatically after about a day.

Cookies and analytics

When you sign in we set a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you logged in. It is needed for the service to work. The site also uses Google Analytics (Google LLC) to measure visits and page usage; Google may set cookies and receive your IP address and browser and device information. You can block this with your browser settings, tracking-protection tools or Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

Why we use it

To create and secure accounts, send verification and security emails, run the referral program and airdrops, detect and prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, understand how the site is used and meet legal obligations. Depending on where you live, our legal bases are performing our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and improving the service, legal obligations, and your consent where consent is required. We do not send marketing messages unless you separately agree to receive them.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare for hosting, our database and security, including the Turnstile CAPTCHA on registration and airdrop pages; Resend to send verification and password-reset emails; Google Analytics for usage statistics; blockchain and market-data providers, to which we send wallet addresses to look up public balances and transactions; and Telegram, on which the airdrop bot runs. If you connect a wallet with WalletConnect, its connection code is loaded from a public CDN and your connection runs through the WalletConnect network. These providers process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies.

Public blockchain data

Wallet addresses and transactions on BNB Smart Chain are public by design. We cannot edit or delete data that is recorded on a public blockchain.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for fraud prevention, handling disputes and meeting legal obligations. Airdrop claim records are kept to enforce the one-claim-per-wallet and one-claim-per-Telegram-account rules. Login sessions expire automatically.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, including outside the EEA. Where this applies, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes and sign-in uses secure, HTTP-only cookies. No online service can be made perfectly secure, so please keep your credentials and wallet safe and never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. To make a request, email info@goldityglobal.com from the email address on your account (for Telegram airdrop data, include your Telegram user ID). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The service is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this notice. The version date at the top shows the current version.
