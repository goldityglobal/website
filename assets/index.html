2. Transparency by Design

Transparency becomes more meaningful when it can be independently verified. The GDTY contract is publicly accessible on BNB Smart Chain, allowing anyone to examine its fundamental characteristics directly.

A defined supply

The entire supply of 19,100,000 GDTY was created once at deployment. The token contract contains no mint function, so additional GDTY cannot be created through the token contract.

Holders may burn their own tokens, allowing the circulating and total supply to decrease over time.

0% token tax

The GDTY token contract applies:

  • 0% buy tax
  • 0% sell tax
  • 0% wallet-to-wallet transfer tax

The contract contains no token-tax logic, blacklist mechanism, pause function or trading restriction.

Network gas fees and fees charged by third-party platforms may still apply independently of the GDTY token contract.

Ownership renounced

GDTY was built using OpenZeppelin's Ownable2Step ownership model.

The contract's only owner-restricted function was used to register the four vesting contracts. That action was designed to be performed once and cannot be executed again.

Ownership of the token contract has since been renounced. As a result, the GDTY token contract currently has no owner and no remaining administrative functions.

This is not something users need to take on trust. It can be independently checked on BscScan by reading owner(), which returns the zero address.

4. Lock and Vesting Schedule

Token locks and vesting schedules are most useful when participants can understand what is locked, when it may become available and how the release mechanism works.

GOLDITY therefore publishes these schedules in advance.

Team Finance lock

6,000,000 GDTY are locked from 14 September 2026 until 28 March 2028.

GOLDITY vesting contracts

Four dedicated GOLDITY vesting contracts were deployed on 6 September 2026 and are fully funded.

These vesting contracts have no owner and no administrative functions. Their core parameters, including token, beneficiary, start time, cliff, duration, interval and amount, were fixed at deployment and cannot be changed.

Only the designated beneficiary can release tokens that have become vested according to the predefined schedule.

5. Wallet and Allocation Disclosure

This wallet also owns the Team Finance lock and deployed the contract that previously held ownership of the GDTY token contract, before ownership was renounced.

6. Market Reference Price

The website's reference price is the arithmetic mean of valid GDTY/USDT prices from the configured Uniswap V2 and PancakeSwap V2 pools.

If one source is unavailable, no reference price is shown until both are valid again.

The displayed value is a reference price, not a guaranteed execution price. Actual market prices can vary according to liquidity, trade size, slippage and market conditions.
