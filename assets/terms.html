Codes may not be sold, spammed, used for deceptive conduct, or used to create fraudulent or duplicate accounts. Self-referral is not permitted. Each reward is held for about 7 days and is paid only if the GDTY purchased stays in the referred account's connected wallets for the entire period: any sale or transfer of GDTY out of those wallets during the period (other than between the account's own connected wallets) cancels the reward for that purchase.
