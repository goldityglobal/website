GOLDITY Whitepaper

GOLDITY (GDTY) is a fixed-supply digital asset deployed on BNB Smart Chain. This document describes the token parameters, contract behavior, token distribution and lock schedule as verified on-chain at the snapshot date. All figures can be verified on BscScan and may change over time.

2. Contract Mechanics

The entire supply was minted once at deployment. The contract has no mint function, so supply can only decrease, because holders may burn their own tokens.

Buy tax, sell tax and wallet-to-wallet transfer tax are all 0%. The contract contains no tax logic, blacklist, pause function or trading restriction.

Ownership uses a two-step transfer process. The current owner is a smart contract address. The owner's only administrative function, registering the four vesting contracts, has been executed and cannot be executed again.

5. Wallet Disclosure

The beneficiary of all four vesting contracts is wallet 0x02e5…c0C8. This wallet also owns the Team Finance lock and deployed the contract that currently owns the token contract. The Advisors and Team allocations are therefore released to this same wallet.

Public offering and liquidity support (0x02e5…c0C8, ≈ 35% of supply): reserved for a planned public offering and liquidity support. The allocation between sale, liquidity and operations, the price mechanism and any release caps or buyer vesting terms will be published before any tokens are sold. No tokens from this wallet will be sold directly into the liquidity pool.

Community programs (0x6D00…8b32, ≈ 10.47% of supply): reserved for community rewards, quests, airdrops and contests. Each distribution will be documented publicly with transaction links. Referral rewards are not part of the program.

Operations (0x57c0…676a and 0x4908…d, ≈ 2.71% of supply): used for operational purposes.

6. Market Reference Price

The website's reference price is the arithmetic mean of valid GDTY/USDT prices from the configured Uniswap V2 and PancakeSwap V2 pools. If one source is unavailable, no reference price is shown until both are valid again.

8. Risks

Digital assets are highly volatile and may lose value. At the snapshot date, market liquidity is low, the number of holders is small, and a limited number of addresses hold most of the supply. Large unlock events are scheduled (see section 4), and sales of unlocked tokens may affect the market price. The contracts have not been audited by an independent third party as of the snapshot date. Smart contracts, wallets, exchanges, RPC providers and third-party data services can fail or be attacked. Users are responsible for verifying addresses, transactions, networks and applicable laws before interacting with the token.
