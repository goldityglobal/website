GOLDITY Whitepaper

GOLDITY (GDTY) is a fixed-supply digital asset deployed on BNB Smart Chain. This document describes the currently verified token parameters, contract behavior, market-data methodology and account/referral framework.

1. Token Overview

Name: GOLDITY
Symbol: GDTY
Network: BNB Smart Chain
Chain ID: 56
Decimals: 18
Total supply: 19,100,000 GDTY

2. Contract

The official GDTY contract address is shown on the dedicated Contract page and should always be verified before interacting with a token.

View Contract →

3. Contract Mechanics

The current contract uses a fixed supply with no mint function. Holders can burn their own tokens. Buy and sell tax are each 0.05%; wallet-to-wallet transfers are 0%. The contract uses two-step ownership and supports tax-exempt pair/vesting addresses according to its deployed logic.

4. Market Reference Price

The website's Reference Price is the arithmetic mean of valid GDTY/USDT prices from the configured Uniswap V2 and PancakeSwap V2 pools. If one source is unavailable, only the valid source is used. If no valid source exists, the site displays unavailable rather than inventing a price.

5. Accounts & Referral Codes

Registered users receive a unique referral code. A new registration may include an existing code, creating referral attribution. The registration system is designed to collect only necessary account information. Any future referral reward must be governed by separately published terms and applicable law.

6. Privacy & Compliance

Personal data is processed according to the site's Privacy Notice. The service should use purpose limitation, data minimisation, retention controls, security safeguards and transparent notices. No government identification document is requested by the standard registration form. 7. Risks

Digital assets can be highly volatile and may lose value. Smart contracts, wallets, bridges, exchanges, RPC providers and third-party data services can fail or be attacked. Users are responsible for checking contract addresses, transaction details, network and applicable laws before interacting.
